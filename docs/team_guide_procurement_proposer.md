# Procurement Proposer — Your Monday morning in HCM

**Live app:** https://asilhcm.onrender.com  
**Your sidebar badge:** Proc. Proposer

## 1. Sign in
Google sign-in. Role: procurement_proposer.

## 2. First screen
**Bills & Procurement** — vendor bills and requisitions.

## 3. Daily tasks

| # | Task | Where to click |
|---|------|----------------|
| 1 | Register a new vendor | **Vendor Supplier Master** → add supplier details |
| 2 | Create a bill | **Bills & Procurement** → **New bill** flow → fill vendor, contract/site, line items |
| 3 | Submit for approval | On draft bill → action **Submit for Approval** (moves to Pending Approval) |
| 4 | Stock / equipment received | **Inventory & Equipment** → add items when bill is goods-related |
| 5 | OCR / verification (if used) | **Bill Verification** → match scanned bill to draft |

## 4. Other modules on your menu
Vendor Supplier Master, Inventory & Equipment, Bill Verification, Accounts Payable (Bills Queue tab only — you land on **Bills Queue**, not Payroll Queue).

## 5. Escalate to
| Issue | Contact |
|-------|---------|
| Approval delayed | **Procurement Approver** or **Procurement Mgr** |
| Wrong contract / client on bill | **Operations** / **Finance Manager** |
| Payment status | **AP Team** (after approval) |
