# AP Team — Your Monday morning in HCM

**Live app:** https://asilhcm.onrender.com  
**Your sidebar badge:** AP Team

## 1. Sign in
Google sign-in. Role: p_team.

## 2. First screen
**Accounts Payable** — default tab **Payroll Queue** (salary bank runs).

## 3. Daily tasks

| # | Task | Where to click |
|---|------|----------------|
| 1 | Pay locked payroll months | **Accounts Payable** → **Payroll Queue** → select month → enter payment ref/date → **Confirm** |
| 2 | Pay approved vendor bills | **Accounts Payable** → **Bills Queue** → confirm payment on approved bills |
| 3 | Check bill status before paying | **Bills & Procurement** → **Active** tab → status must be approved (not Draft) |
| 4 | Payment history | Use ledger views exposed to AP (Payment Ledger API) via finance reports if enabled |

## 4. Other modules on your menu
Bills & Procurement (status visibility).

## 5. Escalate to
| Issue | Contact |
|-------|---------|
| Bill not in queue / wrong amount | **Procurement Proposer** → **Procurement Approver** |
| FM approval required on batch | **Finance Manager** |
| Payroll month not locked | **Finance Approver** (locks in **Payroll Sheet**) |
