# AR Team — Your Monday morning in HCM

**Live app:** https://asilhcm.onrender.com  
**Your sidebar badge:** AR Team

## 1. Sign in
Google sign-in with your assigned work email. Role must be r_team (not pending).

## 2. First screen
HCM opens **Invoices (AR)** — client billing and Xero-related work lives here.

## 3. Daily tasks

| # | Task | Where to click |
|---|------|----------------|
| 1 | Issue or update client invoices | **Invoices (AR)** → create / edit draft → submit for approval path used by Finance Approver |
| 2 | Push approved invoices to Xero | **Invoices (AR)** → select invoice → **Push to Xero** (when status allows) |
| 3 | Track PO vs billing | **PO Tracking** → link POs to invoices where required |
| 4 | Collections view | **AR & Collections** → **AR Aging** table → follow up on overdue buckets |
| 5 | Vendor bills tied to client recovery | **Bills & Procurement** (read/submit paths as per bill) → **Create invoice** from bill when workflow applies |

## 4. Other modules on your menu
Invoices (AR), PO Tracking, Bills & Procurement, Compliance Ledger.

## 5. Escalate to
| Issue | Contact |
|-------|---------|
| Invoice approval / void in Xero | **Finance Approver** or **Super Admin** (void-xero) |
| Contract rates, client master data | **Finance Manager** / **Operations** |
| Payroll lines on invoices wrong | **Finance Proposer** + **Payroll Initiator** |
